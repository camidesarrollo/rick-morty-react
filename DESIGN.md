## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, utilitarian sci-fi catalog aesthetic tailored for deep-space indexing and multiverse character exploration. It balances corporate data density with tactical field-terminal clarity: systematic, analytical, and highly structured, yet energized by luminous bio-luminescent accents.

### Design Movements & Visual Language
- **Systematic Database / Functional Sci-Fi:** Emphasizes crisp compartmentalization, high-legibility information hierarchies, and tactical status markers. The atmosphere evokes an operational galactic research console rather than playful cartoon kitsch.
- **Editorial Cleanliness:** Ample structural whitespace, neutral framing, and subtle boundary rules ensure dense records remain effortless to scan and parse.

### Tone & Sensibility
- **Analytical & Observant:** Communicates authoritative archiving and precision monitoring across infinite realities.
- **Controlled Vibrancy:** Night-sky deep blues anchor primary interactions, while electric alien-lime elements direct focus to critical indicators and primary workflows.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-clean archival environment backed by an ethereal deep navy, grounded by structured neutral borders, and energized by high-vis bio-lime.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#182235`):** Midnight Space. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis text, prominent headers, and high-contrast structural framing.
- **Secondary (`#B7F36B`):** Toxic Lime / Energy Core. High-visibility accent utilized for terminal indicators, primary state highlights, search focuses, and critical telemetry alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#303B4D`):** Deep Hull Slate. Utilized for secondary action states, elevated technical borders, sub-headers, and muted navigational chrome.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#F5F7FA`):** Sub-light sterile background providing low-strain contrast across expansive card grids.
- **Card & Sheet Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-purity white used for individual record containers, modal dialogs, and drawer overlays.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle spatial dividers utilize `#E0E5EC` for standard surface separation, scaling to `#303B4D` for active or technical structural demarcations.

### Character Status Indicators
- **Alive / Operational:** Background `#E5F8D8`, Text/Icon `#28651D`. High-clarity vital signs.
- **Deceased / Terminated:** Background `#FCE8E8`, Text/Icon `#912018`. Urgent anomaly notification.
- **Unknown / Unresolved:** Background `#EEF0F4`, Text/Icon `#475467`. Neutral missing-telemetry tone.

## Typography

The system adopts a pure, single-family typographical execution relying exclusively on **Inter**. Systematic visual hierarchy is achieved strictly through deliberate weight calibration, tracking variations, and rigorous vertical rhythm conforming to a 4px baseline matrix.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Monitors & Section Headers:** Uses tight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) on display and large headlines to project density and authoritative engineering.
- **Body & Dossier Copy:** Rendered in regular weights with neutral tracking to sustain comfortable readability over extended research logs.
- **Labels, Telemetry, & Multiverse Codes:** Uppercase label tiers paired with positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) and semi-bold weights for identifiers, dimension indices, and taxonomy tags.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an exact 4px-increment structural rhythm to enforce an ordered, operational database feel.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive grid with `2rem` outer canvas margin and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Primary character explorer cards occupy 3 or 4 columns depending on density toggle.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Explorer cards default to a 2-column format.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow with `1rem` canvas margin and `1rem` gutters. Cards shift to single-column full-width feeds or compact horizontal layout tiles.

### Spacing Density Principles
- Content gaps inside data items use tight steps (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px) to preserve data cohesion.
- Structural container padding standardizes on `space-md` (16px) for compact telemetry pods and `space-lg` (24px) for prominent character dossier sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the technical sci-fi database identity, visual separation avoids dramatic, soft skeuomorphic drop-shadows. Instead, depth is structured through sharp tonal planes, ghost hairline boundaries, and crisp offset overlays.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Surface `#F5F7FA`. Un-elevated grounding field.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed in an exact 1px hairline border of `#E0E5EC`. Zero diffuse shadow under resting state; hover introduces an ambient lift of `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(24, 34, 53, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Panels & Sticky Controls):** Surface `#FFFFFF` with a refined structural outline (`#E0E5EC`) and focused depth: `0 4px 16px -4px rgba(24, 34, 53, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Portals & Dossier Overlays):** Surface `#FFFFFF` elevated above a backdrop scrim (`rgba(24, 34, 53, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). Shadow defined as `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(24, 34, 53, 0.2)`. Active borders may highlight with `#303B4D`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is set to **Rounded (Level 2)**. Standard interface containers use an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius, maintaining architectural precision without harsh 90-degree corners or overly casual pill formats.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `rounded` (8px / `0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Filter Sheets, Panels:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`).
- **Inspection Modals, Global Drawers:** `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`).
- **Status Dots & Circular Avatars:** Full round (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#182235`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, height 40px, padding `0 1rem`. On hover, background shifts to `#303B4D`. Focus indicator produces a 2px offset ring in `#B7F36B`.
- **Accent / Special Action:** Background `#B7F36B`, text `#182235` (bold weight), border none. Hover scales luminance subtly with `filter: brightness(0.95)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#182235`, border 1px solid `#E0E5EC`. On hover, border color updates to `#303B4D` and background tints to `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards (Character Records)
- Base background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E0E5EC`, radius 16px.
- Card header features an edge-to-edge or 8px-inset character visual with status badge anchored to the top-right corner.
- Body content displays Character Name (`title-lg`, `#182235`), Species / Origin subtitle (`body-sm`, `#475467`), and a dedicated 2-column key-value attribute grid (`Last Known Location`, `Dimension Origin`).
- Interactive hover: Subtle card rise (`translateY(-2px)`), border transition to `#303B4D`, and faint shadow enhancement.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Radius 6px, padding `2px 8px`, typography `label-sm` (uppercase).
  - *Alive:* `#E5F8D8` surface, `#28651D` label with a 6px pulsating `#28651D` circle dot.
  - *Dead:* `#FCE8E8` surface, `#912018` label with a static `#912018` circle dot.
  - *Unknown:* `#EEF0F4` surface, `#475467` label with a neutral ring indicator.
- **Taxonomy Chips (Episode / Dimension):** Surface `#F5F7FA`, border 1px solid `#E0E5EC`, text `#303B4D`, radius 6px.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E0E5EC`, height 40px, padding `0 12px`, radius 8px.
- Placeholder text in `#98A2B3`.
- Active/Focused: Border snaps to `#182235` with an outer glow/ring of 2px `#B7F36B`.
- Integrated terminal prefix: Monospace-styled glyph or icon colored `#303B4D`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base box 18x18px, border 1.5px solid `#303B4D`, radius 4px (checkbox) or circle (radio), background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked state: Background `#182235`, checkmark stroke `#B7F36B` (bold contrast).

### Lists & Key-Value Dossier Sheets
- Grouped tabular records separated by hairline dividers `#E0E5EC`.
- Metric keys rendered in `label-sm`, uppercase, `#475467`.
- Metric values rendered in `body-md`, semi-bold, `#182235`.
